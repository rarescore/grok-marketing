# RS Marketing Animation and Page Map

## Intro: 0–4 seconds

- **0.0–1.4s:** RS Formula car enters from off-screen left. Background barriers and track streak in the opposite direction.
- **1.4–2.1s:** Side-tracking camera stabilizes beside the car. Wheel overlays intensify.
- **2.1–3.3s:** Camera pushes into the rear wheel. The wheel remains centered while the car image scales.
- **2.25–3.7s:** Procedural portal rings appear, RS logo loads from 0–100%.
- **3.7–4.3s:** Controlled white flash transitions into the homepage.
- **Accessibility:** Skip button and reduced-motion automatic bypass.
