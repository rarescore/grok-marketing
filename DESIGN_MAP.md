# Cinematic Design Map

## Scene 01 — The Signal

**DOM message:** We reinvent how brands get found.

**3D motion:** A black metallic signal craft floats just right of center. It responds subtly to the pointer while the camera remains calm. A restrained horizon line replaces a conventional hero photograph.

**Reference principle used:** One large object stays alive at the center of the experience.

## Scene 02 — Visibility Engine

**DOM message:** One system. Every growth channel.

**3D transition:** The craft moves through a converging wire network. A pale single pane of glass slides into frame as the service console becomes visible.

**Reference principle used:** A physical transition object connects the scene and layout instead of a hard section cut.

## Scene 03 — Selected Systems

**DOM message:** Built to look premium. Engineered to convert.

**3D transition:** The glass gate passes the camera. The craft recedes while layered frames and a scan line frame each case-study system.

**Reference principle used:** Minimal project presentation with controlled depth rather than a crowded gallery.

## Scene 04 — Process

**DOM message:** A clear process. No random motion.

**3D transition:** Ten data pillars rise at varying heights while the process steps appear as a disciplined vertical sequence.

**Reference principle used:** Architectural forms represent information and progress.

## Scene 05 — Packages

**DOM message:** One destination. Four levels of support.

**3D transition:** Four dark metallic doors fan outward. The Growth door receives the green signal accent.

**Reference principle used:** Product tiers become spatial objects instead of ordinary pricing cards.

## Scene 06 — Results

**DOM message:** Proof should move. Not just the screen.

**3D transition:** The doors collapse into orbital telemetry. Rings rotate around a central signal core as result modules display the existing RS figures with verification notes.

## Scene 07 — Client Transmissions

**DOM message:** The website feels different. The business should too.

**3D transition:** Signal particles expand into a sparse field while masked reviews arrive as transmission panels.

## Scene 08 — Search Intelligence

**DOM message:** Google needs clarity. People do too.

**3D transition:** The orbital signal becomes an information network while long-form SEO concepts use large editorial panels.

## Scene 09 — Contact

**DOM message:** Become impossible to ignore.

**3D transition:** Multiple rings align into a single portal. The craft crosses the field and exits as the form becomes the only active interface.

# Layout rule

Each chapter has:

- one dominant statement
- one short explanatory paragraph
- one primary interaction
- enough vertical distance for the 3D transition to complete

The full service details, package lists, reviews, and article points are preserved, but placed inside controlled consoles and panels rather than competing with the animation.
